Healthcare Use Cases

🩺 Clinical Decision Support System

Challenge: Physicians need AI assistance analyzing patient data and suggesting treatments, but cloud-based systems create HIPAA compliance risk and PHI data breach exposure.

VeritasCore Solution: Deploy on-premise clinical decision support. AI analyzes EHR data, lab results, imaging, and medical literature using local LLM. Zero PHI egress. Cryptographic audit trail for medical-legal defense.

Result: AI-powered clinical support with complete HIPAA compliance. Improved diagnostic accuracy. No cloud data breach risk. Audit trail for liability protection.

💉 Autonomous Medical Device Control

Challenge: Next-generation medical devices require autonomous AI decision-making. FDA requires predetermined change control and rollback capability per PCCP framework.

VeritasCore Solution: Embed VeritasCore in medical device firmware. Ultra-lightweight 6.8MB footprint fits on device hardware. Versioned self-modification with cryptographic signing satisfies FDA PCCP. Deterministic rollback for patient safety.

Result: FDA-compliant autonomous medical devices. Cryptographic proof of device decisions for regulatory submissions. Safety-critical rollback capability.

🏥 Hospital Operations Optimization

Challenge: Hospitals need AI optimization of staffing, bed management, supply chain, but operational data contains PHI that cannot go to cloud.

VeritasCore Solution: On-premise deployment optimizes operations using de-identified or PHI-containing data safely within hospital infrastructure. AI predicts patient flow, optimizes staffing levels, manages inventory.

Result: AI-powered operational excellence without HIPAA violations. Reduced costs, improved patient outcomes. 81% of organizations report revenue increases.
